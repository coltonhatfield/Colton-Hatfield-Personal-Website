PACERAnonymous · client-side · assumption-driven

PACER is a scenario estimate, not a guarantee, certification, actuarial model, penetration test, or substitute for an organization-specific security assessment. Attackers may act irrationally or with incomplete information.

Model card

How PACER calculates profitability

Core equations

Total value = ransom + accessible cash + (customer records × $7) + intellectual property + other revenueExpected revenue = total value × probability of attack successAttack cost = fixed operating cost + RaaS/operator share + expected enforcement costExpected profit = expected revenue − attack cost

Success probability

The model starts at 56%, the share of surveyed 2026 ransomware attacks that encrypted data among organizations that were attacked. Checked D3FEND techniques receive diminishing credit within Model, Harden, Detect, Isolate, Deceive, and Evict. Breadth across tactics matters, so stacking many techniques in one area cannot substitute for defense in depth.

D3FEND boundary

MITRE explicitly states that D3FEND does not prescribe, prioritize, or characterize the effectiveness of countermeasures. PACER therefore treats the tactic weights and probability reductions as transparent scenario assumptions. It assumes every checked technique is fully and successfully implemented.

Economic boundary

The calculator estimates gross value available to a criminal organization. It ignores separate monetization probabilities, as specified for this first version. Defender recovery costs, downtime, and reputational harm are intentionally excluded because they are not attacker revenue.

Privacy

There is no account, analytics, backend request, storage, export, or share function. Uploaded JSON is parsed in browser memory and disappears when the page is closed or reset.

Evidence register

Observed evidence and modeled assumptions

OBSERVED

56% conditional success anchor

Sophos' 2026 survey reports that 56% of attacks against 2,158 organizations hit by ransomware succeeded in encrypting data. PACER uses this as a conditional baseline, not an annual attack probability.

OBSERVED

$7 customer-account resale value

An ARES 2023 peer-reviewed market study found a $7 median asking price for compromised online accounts; 91% were listed between $1 and $30. PACER applies the median to each affected customer record.

25% RaaS/operator share

MODELED

D3FEND effects and unobserved costs

No public dataset measures a universal success reduction for each D3FEND technique or a complete per-attack criminal cost ledger. Tactic weights, labor, overhead, specialist services, and expected enforcement exposure are PACER assumptions and are shown in every result.
