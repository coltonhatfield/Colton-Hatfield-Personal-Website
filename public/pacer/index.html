PACERAnonymous · client-side · assumption-driven

PACER is a scenario estimate, not a guarantee, certification, actuarial model, penetration test, or substitute for an organization-specific security assessment. Attackers may act irrationally or with incomplete information.

Model card

How PACER calculates profitability

Core equations

Total value = ransom + accessible cash + (customer records × $7) + intellectual property + other revenueExpected revenue = sum(value × channel outcome probability × realization fraction)Attack cost = upfront resources + stage-weighted effort + channel fees + enforcement allowanceExpected profit = expected revenue − attack cost

Conditional impact probability

The 56% reference describes encryption among surveyed ransomware victims. The four stage probabilities and their transfer to this scenario are assumptions, not measured stage rates. Official D3FEND families map defenses to relevant stages, cap overlapping credit and limit cumulative effects. Restoration changes ransom collection, not prior data theft. The impact probability is not the probability of every revenue channel succeeding.

D3FEND boundary

MITRE explicitly states that D3FEND does not prescribe, prioritize, or characterize countermeasure effectiveness. PACER therefore exposes a control-effectiveness setting for deployment scope and operating quality. The result is conditional on an attack occurring and is not an annual probability that the organization will be attacked.

Economic boundary

The calculator estimates one access-broker-mediated criminal operation. Entered values are ceilings, not guaranteed collections; enter distinct revenue sources without overlap. Ransom uses a 48% payment reference, while cash, customer data, IP and other proceeds use explicitly assumed realization fractions of 75%, 35%, 20% and 50%. The 25% RaaS share applies only to ransom, and other proceeds incur an assumed 10% cash-out fee. Defender recovery costs, downtime and reputation are excluded. The profit sensitivity range is an assumption envelope, not a confidence interval.

Privacy

There is no account, analytics, backend request, storage, export, or share function. Uploaded JSON is parsed in browser memory and disappears when the page is closed or reset.

Observed evidence and modeled assumptions

OBSERVED

56% conditional success anchor

Sophos' 2026 survey reports that 56% of attacks against 2,158 organizations hit by ransomware succeeded in encrypting data. PACER uses this as an impact reference, not an annual attack probability or measured rate for an undefended company. The same report found 48% of encrypted victims paid.

OBSERVED

$7 customer-account resale value

An ARES 2023 peer-reviewed market study found a $7 median asking price for compromised online accounts; 91% were listed between $1 and $30. PACER retains $7 as a proxy ceiling and assumes 35% realization. These are not measured prices or sale rates for generic customer records; the sensitivity analysis tests $0.10–$7 proxy values.
